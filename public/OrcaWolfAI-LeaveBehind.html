Carrier Readiness — 5-Minute Demo Cheat Sheet

USS Nimitz (CVN-68) · USS Theodore Roosevelt (CVN-71) · medical and line-of-duty readiness under threat

Before you start

Open ⚓ Carrier in the nav. Optionally open 🛰 Mesh C2 in a second browser tab so the live telemetry panel appears on the Carrier page.

The run of show

  1. 1Frame it. A training demonstrator of what a sovereign mesh could support — synthetic data, no operational feeds.0:30
  2. 2Calibrate. Click ⚙ Calibration Settings. Show Force Readiness (manning and minimum thresholds), Blood Supply (O-Negative alert below the 80-unit MASCAL minimum), MedEvac Assets (lift capacity updates live).1:15
  3. 3Pick the theater. Theater & Echelon tab: choose Strait of Hormuz. The map shows the carrier, the 200 nm CASEVAC ring and Role 2–4 facilities. Click one to select your next echelon of care.1:00
  4. 4Run an exercise. Choose one or both carriers, crew complement, threat (Drone Swarm, Sea-Skimming Missile, EW/GPS Denial, CBRN or Combined Arms) and intensity. Press Run.0:45
  5. 5Read the dials. Eight medical and six line-of-duty dials. Then the department bars and the streaming event log.0:45
  6. 6Give the Commander the answer. Scroll to Recommended Course of Action: P1/P2/P3 actions with numbers from your calibrated assets, plus the top-line call. Show Print C2 Brief.0:45
Say it this way: “Every run is saved with a plain-English summary, so a commander can compare exercises over time. It is a decision-support concept — the data is synthetic, weighted against published NATO STANAG 2122 and Joint Trauma System doctrine.”
